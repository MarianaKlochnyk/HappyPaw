About us

“Purrfect Heaven” is more than just a shelter. It is a chance for a new life for animals who find themselves on the street. We find them in difficult conditions - hungry, frightened, sometimes sick - and give them a second chance. Here, every tail has its own story, every paw is waiting for warmth and care.
